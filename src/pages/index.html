// 03 — Origin

From a team name to a company.

“Dyu” — sky. “Netra” — eye. Dyunetra began as the name of an engineering student team that wanted to see problems clearly and solve them end to end.

  • Team DyunetraEngineering students from Bajaj Institute of Technology, building with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Smart India Hackathon 2022Grand prize winner, Software Edition — on problem statements from the India Meteorological Department and the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
  • Dyunetra, the companyFounded by Yashwardhan Katkamwar to turn that engineering culture into products for security, trust and AI.
