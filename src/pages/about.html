// Our story

The eye of the sky.

Dyunetra — also written Dyu-Netra — began as Team Dyunetra, an engineering student team at the Bajaj Institute of Technology. The name joins dyu (sky) and netra (eye): a wide, patient view that notices what others miss.

In 2022 the team won the national Smart India Hackathon (Software Edition), working on problem statements from the India Meteorological Department and the Ministry of Earth Sciences. The experience set the pattern for what followed: pick a hard problem, build the whole thing, and make it work for real users.

Today Dyunetra is a company led by its founder, Yashwardhan Katkamwar, with three products in market and a consulting practice that helps teams apply the same standards to their own systems.

// Founder

Meet Yashwardhan Katkamwar.

Founder · Dyunetra

Yashwardhan Katkamwar is the founder of Dyunetra. He started as part of Team Dyunetra, the Bajaj Institute of Technology student team that won the Smart India Hackathon 2022 (Software Edition).

He is a multidisciplinary professional whose expertise spans cybersecurity, digital forensics, case investigation, business development and multiple programming languages — combining technical proficiency, analytical thinking and business acumen to bridge security, technology and strategy. In consulting practice he has worked as a Senior Cyber Security Consultant, delivering practical solutions for complex client challenges.

At Dyunetra he leads product direction for MEVONU, QEVRAXIS and DevAI, with a consistent focus on verifiable evidence, explainable intelligence and governed automation.
