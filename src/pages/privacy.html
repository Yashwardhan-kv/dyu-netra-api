Privacy policy.

We keep data collection minimal. This page explains what we touch, why, and the controls you have.

1. What we collect

We do not run advertising trackers or sell data. The only information we process is:

  • Contact form submissions — the name, email, organisation and message you provide, used solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • Server logs — standard web-server logs (IP address, user agent, requested pages) retained briefly for security and abuse prevention.

2. How we use it

Information you send us is used to reply to you and, where relevant, to prepare proposals or demonstrations of our products (MEVONU, QEVRAXIS, DevAI). We do not share it with third parties except infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate this site and our email.

3. Cookies

This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. A session cookie may be set by the hosting provider's protection layer purely to validate that requests come from a real browser.

4. Retention & your rights

Enquiry correspondence is retained only as long as useful for our engagement. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by writing to founder@dyunetra.dpdns.org.

5. Security

We apply transport encryption, restrictive security headers and least-privilege infrastructure practices. No system is perfectly secure, but we design ours to minimise what can be exposed in the first place.
